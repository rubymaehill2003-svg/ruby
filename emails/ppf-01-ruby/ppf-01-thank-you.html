A few tips before
your order arrives
REED DIFFUSERS
Flip your reeds daily for the first week, then weekly – or whenever you’d like to refresh the scent.
CANDLES
Let the top layer melt edge to edge before blowing it out, and trim the wick to 5mm before each burn.
PLACEMENT
Keep diffusers away from sunny window sills – sunlight and heat can change the oil.
